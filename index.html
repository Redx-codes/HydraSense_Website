Scan to open HydraSense

One QR for everyone: it opens this website, where all measured spots are shown on the map.

HydraSense is a screening tool, not a certified laboratory test, and it does not purify water. Every value is labelled Measured (sensor), Estimated (calculated) or Manual test (test kit). The index and limits follow BIS IS 10500:2012; bacteria, heavy metals and pesticides are not measured by the sensors.
